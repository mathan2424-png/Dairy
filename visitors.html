Visitor Profiles

Connecting decision-makers, professionals, and farmers to the latest in dairy innovation.

KEY ATTENDEES

Key Visitor Profiles

Visitors at India Dairy Show are diverse, comprising dairy farmers, processors, equipment manufacturers, investors, researchers, and government officials looking for technology, networking, and business opportunities. Key attendees include decision- makers, veterinarians, distributors, and supply chain managers aiming to improve efficiency and adopt new processing techniques.

Producers & Farmers

Dairy farm owners, livestock keepers, and commercial progressive farmers looking for modern, efficient farming techniques.

Dairy Processors & Technologists

Milk processing plant owners, dairy product manufacturers, and food technologists seeking new machinery and quality control equipment.

Industry Professionals

Veterinarians, cattle feed manufacturers, animal healthcare providers, and supply chain managers.

Business & Trade

Equipment distributors, packaging professionals, traders, dealers, and agents aiming to improve efficiency.

Investors & Decision Makers

Dairy sector investors, corporate executives, company promoters, and financial institutions looking for investment opportunities.

Research & Policy

Dairy scientists, researchers, students, and government officials from agricultural departments.

VISITOR GOALS

Primary Objectives of Visitors

Networking

Building business-to-business (B2B), B2C, or B2G connections with suppliers and manufacturers.

Technology Sourcing

Identifying new equipment for dairy processing and farming advancement to improve productivity.

Knowledge Sharing

Engaging with research papers and market trends in packaging and cold storage infrastructure.

Leading the transformation of South Indian dairy industry through innovation and collaboration.
