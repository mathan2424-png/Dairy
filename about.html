ABOUT US

Prompt Trade Fairs India Private Limited

India Dairy Show 2027: India Dairy Show 1st Edition welcomes your interest for an unparalleled opportunity to collaborate, explore, and connect to the future of the dairy industry. The India Dairy Show 1st Edition assures to be the epicenter of dairy innovation, collaboration, and growth.

India Dairy Show is rapidly emerging as one of the largest and most influential South India dairy summits, surpassing renowned events like the Tamil Nadu Cooperative Milk Producers Federation Limited (TCMPF) - AAVIN. While focusing on innovation, sustainability, and regional market trends, it stands out due to its sheer market size, consumption patterns, and rapid industry transformation.

With India being the largest producer and consumer of dairy, the country's dairy sector is witnessing unprecedented growth, driven by urbanisation, rising disposable income, evolving consumer preferences, and a strong emphasis on health and nutrition.

India Dairy Show is setting the stage for South Indian dairy leadership, offering insights, partnerships, and innovations that will shape the future of dairy worldwide. Expanding cold chain infrastructure, increasing private sector investments, and a growing appetite for value-added dairy products like ice creams, cheese, and sweets further strengthen India's position as the global dairy leader.

Given that milk and dairy are an intrinsic part of Indian food culture, India Dairy Show will become the ultimate platform for industry leaders, innovators, and stakeholders, offering unmatched business opportunities and insights into the future of South Indian dairy.
